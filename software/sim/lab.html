What is not proven

Only directional yaw is physically fitted. The rover lacks the range and odometry observations required to deploy this policy.

What fitting changed

Real2Sim versus Sim2Real

Real2Sim identifies simulator parameters from physical logs. Sim2Real tests whether a controller developed under randomized simulated parameters still succeeds on hardware.

What runs on the Jetson

A Linux user service launches a Python process. Python owns orchestration; TensorRT executes YOLO on the NVIDIA GPU; OpenCV performs feature tracking on the CPU.

frontier-autonomy.service→ autonomy_service.py→ TensorRTYoloDetector.detect(JPEG)→ TargetController.update(observation)→ plan_execution(action)→ Pi /api/action or /api/heading/turn→ watchdog → PWM → TB6612 → motors

Why two computers?

The Jetson handles expensive perception and planning. The Pi owns GPIO and the fail-closed watchdog. If the Jetson or network disappears, the Pi stops refreshing PWM within 350 ms.

Visual motion now

ORB finds distinctive image patches. Hamming matching pairs them between frames. RANSAC rejects inconsistent pairs and fits a homography. This gives pixel motion and image rotation, but no metres until calibration or another displacement sensor supplies scale.

What to learn next

  1. Coordinate frames: pixels, camera rays, body, odometry, and map.
  2. Calibration: camera geometry, gyro bias, wheel radius, track width, and deadband.
  3. Estimation: uncertainty, filtering, visual-inertial odometry, and loop closure.
  4. Mapping: inverse sensor models, log odds, occupancy grids, and topological maps.
  5. Planning: A*, frontiers, collision inflation, trajectories, and recovery.
  6. Control: PWM, feedback, PID, saturation, latency, and actuator dynamics.
  7. Deployment: systemd, telemetry, replay, fault injection, and interfaces.
  8. Production: ROS 2, C++, deterministic controllers, lifecycle, and safety cases.

The hardware truth

Camera plus IMU support object detection, rotation, and experimental visual memory. Reliable metric mapping and house-scale obstacle avoidance require range or depth and preferably wheel encoders.

Transfer to robot arms

The architecture is the same: sensors estimate state, perception identifies geometry, a planner selects a collision-free trajectory, a controller tracks it, and a safety layer rejects stale commands.
